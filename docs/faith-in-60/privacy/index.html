Your progress, your device

Privacy policy

Faith in 60 is provided by Michael Abadi Santoso and is designed for a quiet, personal moment with Scripture. No account is needed, and your story progress stays on your device unless you choose to share an activity summary.

Michael Abadi Santoso · Effective 8 October 2026

What the app stores

The app stores your completed stories, completion dates and times, active visit durations, replay status, and the state needed to resume an unfinished story. An unfinished session can include its current step, attempt count, and challenge-feedback result.

It also stores which stories you have saved and local settings, such as whether you have completed the welcome screen. The seven-day activity calendar is calculated from completed visits. Practice answers are not saved and Practice does not count as a completed story visit.

The app does not ask you to enter personal reflections or prayer text. It has no advertising, integrated analytics service, or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

Local storage and backups

The included stories, illustrations, and Bible passages work offline. The app does not automatically send your progress or saved-story list to us, and it does not provide an account-based cloud synchronization service.

App data may be included in your device backups depending on your Apple settings. Those backups are managed through Apple’s services and your device settings.

Sharing is your choice

Story sharing and activity-summary sharing use the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination. The activity summary can be previewed before sharing and contains completed story identifiers, completion dates, active durations, and replay information.

It excludes selected answers, personal reflections, and your saved-story list. Nothing is sent automatically. A recipient or service you choose handles shared information under its own practices.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the contact address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request. You do not need to send an activity report or private reflections to receive help.

Our email service processes correspondence to deliver and store it. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to respond to the request, investigate related problems, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion using the contact address below. If information must be retained, we will explain the reason when responding.

Resetting and removing data

“Reset my journey” archives your current progress and saved stories locally before starting again. It does not erase that archive. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app can preserve data. Existing device backups must be managed separately in your Apple settings.

We cannot erase local progress or device backups remotely. You can ask us to delete information you have sent to support by emailing the address below.

Web pages and purchases

Publisher, support, and privacy links open web pages outside the offline story experience. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as an IP address and browser request, under the GitHub privacy statement. Other websites follow their own policies.

Our support and privacy pages have no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies placed by us. Third-party sites follow their own policies.

Apple handles App Store purchases through your Apple Account. The app does not ask for or receive your payment-card details. Apple’s services and device backups follow Apple’s privacy policy.

Audience and policy updates

Faith in 60 is designed for adults and young adults. It does not request a date of birth or create user accounts. If the app’s practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
