Terms of use

Faith in 60 is an illustrated collection of seven Gospel discoveries for iPhone, provided by Michael Abadi Santoso.

Effective 8 October 2026

Apple’s Standard EULA

App Store downloads of Faith in 60 use Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Read that agreement for the license terms that apply to using the app. This page provides product information and links to the agreement; it does not replace it with a custom license.

The included collection

The app includes seven illustrated Gospel stories, interactive challenges, explanations, optional reflections, and the selected full Bible passages. Library, saved stories, Practice and the activity calendar support revisiting the collection.

The short core is designed to take about a minute. Full passages and personal reflection can take longer. This is not a full Bible or a promise of new daily content.

Purchase

Faith in 60 is offered as a paid download with all included stories and app features covered by that purchase. There are no in-app purchases or recurring subscriptions. Apple displays the current price and applicable purchase terms before purchase.

Apple handles billing. For a refund request, follow Apple’s purchase-support instructions. Eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable consumer rights.

Scripture and editorial writing

Scripture is quoted from the World English Bible Classic, a public-domain translation. Original summaries, explanations, illustrations and reflection prompts are separate from the quoted Scripture.

If you find a quotation or interpretation concern, send the story title, passage reference and exact wording to support so it can be reviewed.

Progress and privacy

Progress and saved stories remain on your device unless you choose to share an activity summary. Practice answers are not saved. Resetting the journey preserves an archive locally; deleting the app removes local app data. Device backups are managed separately.

Read the privacy policy for details about local storage, sharing, support messages and this website.
