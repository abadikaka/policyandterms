Common questions

What is included?

Seven illustrated Gospel stories, observation and sequencing challenges, explanations, optional reflections, and the full selected Bible passages. You can search the Library, save stories, revisit completed challenges in Practice, and see completed visits on a seven-day activity calendar.

This is a small collection to revisit at your pace. It does not include the whole Bible or a promise of new daily content. There is no recurring subscription or in-app purchase.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. The stories, illustrations, passages, Library, Practice, and local progress work offline. Publisher and support links, sending email, and online sharing services may require a connection.

How do I open the next story?

Finish the current discovery. The next story becomes available immediately; you do not need to wait for another day. Completed stories remain available to revisit.

Can I read a passage before unlocking its story?

Yes. Open the full passage from the Library. The included Scripture is available even when its interactive story is not yet unlocked in the sequential journey.

How do saved stories work?

Save a story in the Library so it is easy to find again. Your saved-story list stays on this device and is excluded from the optional activity summary. Saving a story does not bypass the journey’s order.

How does Practice work?

Complete a discovery first, then Practice can revisit up to three challenges from your completed stories. Read their explanations at your own pace, with no score. Practice answers are not saved, and Practice does not add a completed visit to the activity calendar.

Can I read the explanation without answering?

Yes. Use “Show the explanation” during a story challenge. Reflection is optional and ungraded.

Why is my progress different on another device?

Progress and saved stories are local. The app has no account-based synchronization. Device backup behavior depends on your Apple settings.

How do I restart or remove local progress?

Open “About Faith in 60” and choose “Reset my journey.” Confirming “Archive and reset” starts again and keeps the old progress and saved-story archive on the device. This is not a deletion of the archived data.

To remove local app data, delete the app rather than offload it. Existing device backups must be managed separately in your Apple settings.

I found a Scripture or interpretation concern

Send the story title, passage reference, and exact wording you would like us to review. Scripture is quoted from the World English Bible Classic. Summaries, explanations, and reflections are separate editorial writing.

Where can I get purchase help?

For App Store billing or refund requests, follow Apple’s purchase-support instructions. Apple determines refund eligibility. For help using the app, email the contact above.
