Sources & editorial notes

Amitaba’s initial library explores 100 passages from the Dhammapada, an early Buddhist Pali verse collection. The optional Eightfold Path practice draws on SN 45.8. This is a focused introduction, not a survey of every Buddhist tradition or a Pure Land curriculum.

The included Pali passages are reproduced unchanged from SuttaCentral’s published root-text files, whose publication record identifies the Pali text as public domain. The pinned source files and attribution notices are bundled. No modern SuttaCentral translation is included. English, Bahasa Indonesia, and Hindi summaries, explanations, and practice prompts are original learning notes, not verbatim translations. Open a lesson’s source panel for its full included Pali passage, reference, and context. Consult a reliable edition and a qualified teacher for deeper study.

Illustrations are symbolic landscapes and objects, not historical reconstructions. Observation questions do not alter the source teachings. The quiet pause and reflections are optional and ungraded; they do not measure spiritual attainment or provide health treatment. The publisher reviews release content; we do not claim independent scholarly endorsement. Send corrections with the lesson title and exact wording to abadi0067@gmail.com.
