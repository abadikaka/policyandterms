Amitaba membership terms · 15 October 2026

Amitaba offers 100 Dhammapada-based lessons in English, Bahasa Indonesia, and Hindi, with short challenges, explanations, optional reflections, and symbolic illustrations. The US storefront price is US$29 per year. The price shown by the App Store for your region applies. The full annual amount is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. There is no free trial. The subscription renews automatically each year unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account to recover access on another supported device. Learning progress is local and does not sync between devices. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its policies and applicable law.

You can use bundled lessons offline while a verified subscription remains active. Purchasing, restoring, and downloading updates need an internet connection. If access expires, your local progress is kept. We may correct or update lessons and artwork to improve accuracy. No spiritual outcome is guaranteed.
