Wise effort · Dhp 68

A deed you can welcome

The verse describes a well-done action as one that does not later bring remorse, and whose fruit can be received with gladness.

A closer look

  1. Freedom from later remorse

This invites attention to consequences, not just immediate excitement. It is a reflection on conduct, not a promise of effortless outcomes.
