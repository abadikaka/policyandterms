Kindness · Dhp 197

A choice within hostility

The verse imagines living without hatred even among people who hate. Our surroundings need not dictate the quality of every response.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What remains possible in a hostile setting?

A closer look

  1. Choosing not to add hatred

Not adding hatred is different from staying in danger. Protection and seeking support can be part of a careful response.
