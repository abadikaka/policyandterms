Wise effort · Dhp 116

Do not postpone the good

The verse encourages prompt good action and restraint from harmful action. Delay can let the mind settle into an unhelpful habit.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What should not be needlessly postponed?

A closer look

  1. A wholesome action

The invitation is to act when a useful opportunity is clear. Careful thought still matters; urgency need not become impulsiveness.
