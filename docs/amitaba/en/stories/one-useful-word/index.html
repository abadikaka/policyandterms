Speech & listening · Dhp 100

One useful sentence

The verse values a meaningful saying that brings peace over a thousand empty words. Length alone does not make speech helpful.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What matters more than the number of words?

A closer look

  1. Their meaning and calming effect

Useful speech is measured by what it communicates, not its size. Brevity helps only when it carries meaning.
