Change & letting go · Dhp 277

Seeing change clearly

The verse states that conditioned things are impermanent. Seeing this with wisdom belongs to the path of purification.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What characteristic is emphasized?

A closer look

  1. Impermanence

Impermanence concerns things arising through conditions. Noticing change can loosen clinging without denying the value of care.
