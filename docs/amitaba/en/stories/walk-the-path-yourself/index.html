The path · Dhp 276

The teacher points; you practice

The verse says the awakened ones show the way, while practitioners must make the effort themselves. Guidance and personal practice have different roles.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What must the practitioner do?

A closer look

  1. Make the effort to practice

A good guide matters, but hearing a direction is not the same as walking it. Practice turns learning into a lived response.
