Speech & listening · Dhp 51–52

Words with fragrance

Beautiful words without matching action are compared to a flower without fragrance. Words joined to practice are like a flower with both color and scent.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What gives the words their fragrance?

A closer look

  1. Action that matches them

The contrast is between appearance and lived conduct. One kept promise can make a good statement meaningful.
