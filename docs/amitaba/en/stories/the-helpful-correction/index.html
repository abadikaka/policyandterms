Friendship · Dhp 76

A guide to hidden treasure

A wise person who points out a fault is compared to a guide revealing treasure. Helpful correction can become a resource for growth.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is compared to finding treasure?

A closer look

  1. Receiving wise correction

The verse values wise guidance, not humiliation or abuse. A useful correction identifies something we can work on.
