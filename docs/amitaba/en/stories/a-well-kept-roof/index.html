Intentions · Dhp 13–14

A roof for the mind

Rain enters a poorly thatched house. The verses compare this with passion entering an untrained mind, and contrast it with a carefully cultivated mind.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which image represents a cultivated mind?

A closer look

  1. A roof that keeps out rain

The roof image points to ongoing care. Mental training grows through practice, not a demand never to have a difficult feeling.
