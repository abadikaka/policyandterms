Change & letting go · Dhp 170

A bubble and a mirage

The verse invites seeing the world through the images of a bubble and a mirage. What seems solid and lasting deserves closer examination.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What do the images call into question?

A closer look

  1. Assumptions of lasting solidity

These contemplative images loosen clinging. They do not mean that suffering or our responsibilities can simply be ignored.
