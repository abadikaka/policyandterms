Change & letting go · Dhp 278

What cannot fully satisfy

The verse describes conditioned things as dukkha: unable to provide an entirely secure, lasting satisfaction. Wisdom examines that limitation.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is the lesson inviting us to examine?

A closer look

  1. The limits of conditioned satisfaction

Dukkha is broader than an unpleasant moment. The teaching does not deny ordinary joy; it questions relying on changing things for complete security.
