Generosity & conduct · Dhp 103

A different victory

The verse compares conquering many people in battle with mastering oneself. It calls the inward victory the greater one.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which victory is valued more?

A closer look

  1. Mastering oneself

The battle comparison redirects effort toward one's own harmful impulses. It does not invite violence against oneself or others.
