Generosity & conduct · Dhp 165

Your part cannot be delegated

The verse places doing harm, abandoning harm, and purification within one's own conduct. Another person cannot perform that inner work on one's behalf.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What cannot simply be delegated?

A closer look

  1. The work of changing one's conduct

Teachers and friends can help, but they cannot make our choices for us. Responsibility is compatible with asking for help.
