Generosity & conduct · Dhp 84

Success without unfair means

The verse describes a wise person who will not seek advantage for themselves or others through unjust means. A desired outcome does not erase how it is pursued.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is rejected?

A closer look

  1. Unjust means for personal advantage

The verse brings means and ends together. Supporting family or colleagues does not require dishonesty on their behalf.
