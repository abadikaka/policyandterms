Change & letting go · Dhp 151

What we choose to cultivate

Decorated royal chariots wear out, and the body ages. The verse contrasts that change with the enduring value of the good person's teaching and conduct.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does the contrast invite us to value?

A closer look

  1. Goodness beyond outward splendor

The verse turns attention from impressive appearances to what is worth cultivating. Aging is not a failure of practice.
