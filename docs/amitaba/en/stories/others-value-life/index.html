Kindness · Dhp 129–130

Others value life too

These verses ask us to recognize others through our own fear of harm and love of life. That recognition supports not killing or causing killing.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

A closer look

  1. Recognizing that others value life

The appeal is to shared vulnerability. It asks us to widen care beyond our own convenience.

Dhammapada • Dhp 129–130

Sabbe tasanti daṇḍassa, sabbe bhāyanti maccuno; Attānaṁ upamaṁ katvā, na haneyya na ghātaye. Sabbe tasanti daṇḍassa, sabbesaṁ jīvitaṁ piyaṁ; Attānaṁ upamaṁ katvā, na haneyya na ghātaye.
