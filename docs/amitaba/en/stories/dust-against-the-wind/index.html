Generosity & conduct · Dhp 125

Dust against the wind

Throwing fine dust against the wind sends it back toward the thrower. The verse uses this image for harming an innocent person.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does the returning dust represent?

A closer look

  1. The harm of one's own wrongful conduct

The focus is the wrongdoer's responsibility. The image should not be used to claim that every misfortune is deserved.
