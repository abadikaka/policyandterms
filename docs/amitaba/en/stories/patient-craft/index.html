Intentions · Dhp 33

The craft of attention

An arrow maker straightens an arrow. This verse uses that patient craft to picture working with a wavering, hard-to-guard mind.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does the craft image emphasize?

A closer look

  1. Patient training

A wandering mind can be worked with. The image invites skill and repetition rather than anger at oneself.
