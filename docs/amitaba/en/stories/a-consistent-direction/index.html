The path · Dhp 226

A consistent direction

The verse describes people who remain wakeful, train continually, and incline toward Nibbāna. Practice has continuity rather than being only an occasional display.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What gives the practice its direction?

A closer look

  1. Steady training toward liberation

Day and night expresses sustained dedication. It is not an instruction to deprive yourself of sleep or ignore your limits.
