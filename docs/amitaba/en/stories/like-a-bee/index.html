Kindness · Dhp 49

Take care where you visit

A bee takes nectar without damaging a flower's color or scent. The verse offers this image for a sage moving through a village.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is emphasized about the bee?

A closer look

  1. Taking without damaging

The image concerns a monastic's gentle presence. An everyday application is to notice what our use of a place leaves behind.
