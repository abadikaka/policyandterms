Wise effort · Dhp 65

Taste rather than touch

A receptive person can understand a teaching after brief contact with the wise. The verse compares this with a tongue tasting soup.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does the tongue image emphasize?

A closer look

  1. Receptive understanding

The verse values the quality of engagement. A short encounter can matter when we really take in what is taught.
