Intentions · Dhp 50

Look at your own part

This verse redirects attention from other people's faults and unfinished tasks toward what we ourselves have done or left undone.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Where should the examination begin?

A closer look

  1. With our own actions and omissions

Self-examination can make correction fairer. It does not mean overlooking harm or never holding others accountable.
