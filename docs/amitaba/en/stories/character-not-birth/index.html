Generosity & conduct · Dhp 393

Character, not birth

The verse says a person is not a brahmin through hairstyle, family, or birth. It places truth and upright conduct at the center of that spiritual description.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is emphasized over family background?

A closer look

  1. Truth and upright conduct

Here the Dhammapada uses brahmin as a spiritual description of excellence. The lesson does not assign people's worth by caste.
