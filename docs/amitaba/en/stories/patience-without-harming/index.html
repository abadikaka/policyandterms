Kindness · Dhp 184

Patience has a direction

The verse praises patient endurance while rejecting harm and abuse as the conduct of a true renunciant. Patience belongs with non-harming.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which conduct fits the verse?

A closer look

  1. Patience without harming others

This is a standard for a renunciant's conduct. For everyday practice, patience can mean responding firmly without retaliation.
