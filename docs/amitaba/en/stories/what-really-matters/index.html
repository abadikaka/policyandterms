Dhammapada • Dhp 11–12

Asāre sāramatino, sāre cāsāradassino; Te sāraṁ nādhigacchanti, micchāsaṅkappagocarā. Sārañca sārato ñatvā, asārañca asārato; Te sāraṁ adhigacchanti, sammāsaṅkappagocarā.

Passage context

Dhammapada 11–12, an early Pali verse collection preserved in the Theravāda canon. These are original learning notes, not a translation of the quoted Pali or a claim to represent every Buddhist tradition.
