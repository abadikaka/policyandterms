Intentions · Dhp 19–20

Counting someone else's cattle

A person may recite many teachings yet never live them. The verses compare that person to a herder counting another's cattle. A little learning put into practice matters.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is the contrast about?

A closer look

  1. Recitation and lived practice

Knowing words is useful, but these verses ask whether greed, hatred, and confusion are being relinquished.

Dhammapada • Dhp 19–20

Bahumpi ce saṁhita bhāsamāno, Na takkaro hoti naro pamatto; Gopova gāvo gaṇayaṁ paresaṁ, Na bhāgavā sāmaññassa hoti. Appampi ce saṁhita bhāsamāno, Dhammassa hoti anudhammacārī; Rāgañca dosañca pahāya mohaṁ, Sammappajāno suvimuttacitto; Anupādiyāno idha vā huraṁ vā, Sa bhāgavā sāmaññassa hoti. Yamakavaggo paṭhamo.
