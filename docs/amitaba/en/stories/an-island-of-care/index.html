Careful attention · Dhp 25

Build an island

The verse pictures a wise person making an island that a flood cannot overwhelm. Effort, heedfulness, restraint, and training are its foundations.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What builds the island in this image?

A closer look

  1. Effort and careful self-training

The island is an image of cultivated steadiness, not a guarantee that difficulties will never arise.
