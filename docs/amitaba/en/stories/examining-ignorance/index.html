The path · Dhp 243

The deeper obscurity

After describing various stains, the verse names ignorance as the deepest one. It urges practitioners to work toward removing it.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What receives particular emphasis?

A closer look

  1. Ignorance

Here ignorance concerns not seeing clearly in the Buddhist path. The lesson invites examination, not contempt for people who know less.
