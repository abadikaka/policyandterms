Speech & listening · Dhp 258

Wisdom has no word count

Speaking a great deal does not by itself make someone wise. The verse points instead to patience and freedom from hostility and fear.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which quality is a sign of wisdom here?

A closer look

  1. Patient, non-hostile conduct

Fluent speech can be impressive without being wise. The verse asks us to notice the character behind it.
