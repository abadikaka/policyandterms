Friendship · Dhp 330

Alone without resentment

This verse pictures an elephant moving alone in the forest. Solitude is paired with few wants and avoiding harmful conduct.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What accompanies solitude in the verse?

A closer look

  1. Few wants and non-harming

Being alone is not automatically wise. The verse gives solitude an ethical direction rather than praising withdrawal for its own sake.
