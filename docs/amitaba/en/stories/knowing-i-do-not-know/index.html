Wise effort · Dhp 63

Room to learn

Someone who recognizes their lack of understanding has begun to understand something important. Thinking oneself wise while remaining confused closes that opening.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which attitude leaves room for learning?

A closer look

  1. Recognizing what one does not know

Humility is not calling yourself worthless. It means being honest about what still needs examination.
