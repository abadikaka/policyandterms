Speech & listening · Dhp 227–228

Approval is never complete

The verses observe that people criticize silence, much speaking, and little speaking. No one receives only blame or only praise.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does this observation challenge?

A closer look

  1. The hope of pleasing everyone

Universal approval is an unreliable goal. We can still examine criticism and choose words responsibly.
