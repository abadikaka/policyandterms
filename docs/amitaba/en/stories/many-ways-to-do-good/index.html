Wise effort · Dhp 53

Many flowers, many possibilities

Many garlands can be made from a heap of flowers. The verse uses this image to encourage many good actions during a human life.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What do the many garlands represent?

A closer look

  1. Many opportunities for good action

Goodness need not wait for one impressive occasion. Ordinary opportunities can be taken up in many different ways.
