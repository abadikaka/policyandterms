Everyday practice · Dhp 252

The fault we hide

The verse says another person's faults are easy to see, while one's own can be hidden away. It compares this hiding to concealing a bad throw in a game.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What difficulty is highlighted?

A closer look

  1. Seeing one's own faults honestly

The image asks for honesty about our blind spots. It is not a reason to excuse harmful conduct by others.
