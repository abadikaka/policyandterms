Intentions · Dhp 3–4

The repeated grievance

These paired verses describe rehearsing an injury again and again. Holding tightly to that account keeps hatred alive; loosening the grudge allows it to settle.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What keeps hatred going in this contrast?

A closer look

  1. Clinging to the grievance

Letting go of a grudge does not require denying harm, trusting an unsafe person, or abandoning boundaries.
