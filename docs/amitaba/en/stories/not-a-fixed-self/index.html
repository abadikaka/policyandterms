Everyday practice · Dhp 279

Not a fixed possession

The verse states that all dhammas are not-self. Seeing this with wisdom challenges taking experience as a permanent, fully controllable identity.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which assumption is questioned?

A closer look

  1. A permanent self that owns and controls experience

Not-self is a Buddhist teaching, not a claim that people are worthless or that actions do not matter. It calls for careful study.
