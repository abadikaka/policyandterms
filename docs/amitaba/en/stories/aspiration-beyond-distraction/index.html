The path · Dhp 218

An aspiration worth tending

The verse describes a person drawn toward the ineffable goal, with a mind not entangled in sensual attachment. The direction of aspiration matters.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is the aspiration directed toward?

A closer look

  1. Liberation beyond sensual entanglement

The verse's goal is Nibbāna. A small daily practice can honor that direction without claiming the attainment described.
