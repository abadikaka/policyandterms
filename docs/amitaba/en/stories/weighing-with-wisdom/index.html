Everyday practice · Dhp 268–269

Silence is not the whole practice

Simply remaining silent does not make someone a sage. The verses compare the wise person to someone using a balance to discern and reject harm.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What distinguishes the sage in this image?

A closer look

  1. Discernment about good and harm

Quiet can support practice, but the passage asks what understanding accompanies it. Wisdom includes careful discrimination.

Dhammapada • Dhp 268–269
