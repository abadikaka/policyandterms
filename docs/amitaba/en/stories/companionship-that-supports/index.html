Friendship · Dhp 206–207

Company that supports the path

The verses contrast the strain of unwise company with the joy of living among the wise. Companionship can support or complicate practice.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does wise companionship offer?

A closer look

  1. Support and ease in practice

Good company does not make practice unnecessary. It offers conditions in which learning and care are easier to sustain.
