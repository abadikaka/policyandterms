Friendship · Dhp 208

Follow a reliable example

The verse recommends keeping company with a wise, learned, patient, and responsible person, like the moon following its course among the stars.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which qualities make the example reliable?

A closer look

  1. Wisdom, patience, and responsibility

A teacher's conduct matters alongside knowledge. We can learn from a good example without surrendering careful examination.
