Everyday practice · Dhp 163

Easy is not always helpful

The verse observes that harmful, self-defeating actions can be easy, while beneficial conduct can be difficult. Ease is not the only guide.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What should not be mistaken for goodness?

A closer look

  1. Ease alone

This is an invitation to choose with discernment. A difficult action is not automatically good, either; its effects matter.
