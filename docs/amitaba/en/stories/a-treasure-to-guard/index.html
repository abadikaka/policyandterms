Careful attention · Dhp 26

A treasure worth guarding

The wise guard heedfulness as someone might guard a precious treasure. Carelessness is contrasted with this deliberate protection.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is the treasure in this comparison?

A closer look

  1. Heedfulness

Attention can be protected by the choices we make around it. The verse values careful living more than display.
