Everyday practice · Dhp 290

A larger kind of happiness

The verse advises letting go of a small pleasure when doing so reveals a greater happiness. Discernment considers more than immediate attraction.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What guides the choice?

A closer look

  1. A greater, wiser happiness

This is a comparison of consequences, not a rule that enjoyment is always wrong. A worthwhile trade can be made deliberately.
