Speech & listening · Dhp 217

Truth belongs with conduct

This verse joins truthfulness with virtue, discernment, and doing one's proper work. An honest sentence belongs within a whole way of living.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What accompanies truthfulness in the verse?

A closer look

  1. Good conduct and discernment

Truthfulness is not separated from character. Being accurate can go together with being considerate and responsible.
