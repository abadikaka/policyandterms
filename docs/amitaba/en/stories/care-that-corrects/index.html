Friendship · Dhp 77

Care can include correction

Advising, teaching, and discouraging harmful conduct may not please everyone. The verse treats such guidance as valuable to those who welcome goodness.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What kind of guidance is valued?

A closer look

  1. Guidance that discourages harmful conduct

Kindness need not mean endorsing everything. Correction can be honest, proportionate, and offered with respect.
