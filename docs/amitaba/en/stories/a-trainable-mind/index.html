Intentions · Dhp 35

Light and restless

The mind can be quick and difficult to restrain, moving wherever it wishes. This verse says training it supports happiness.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is recommended for the restless mind?

A closer look

  1. Training it

The verse treats the mind as trainable. A brief pause can begin that work without promising instant calm.
