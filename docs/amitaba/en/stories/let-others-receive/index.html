Everyday practice · Dhp 249–250

Let others receive

The verses describe how resentment about what others receive disturbs peace. Letting go of that resentment permits greater ease.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What disturbs the mind here?

A closer look

  1. Resentment over others' gifts

The focus is envy, not ignoring unfairness. We can question injustice without resenting another person's nourishment or support.

Dhammapada • Dhp 249–250
