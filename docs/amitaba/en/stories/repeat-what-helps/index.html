Wise effort · Dhp 118

Let a good action grow

When a person does something good, the verse encourages doing it again and finding joy in it. Repetition can strengthen a wholesome direction.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is encouraged after a good action?

A closer look

  1. Repeat it and appreciate it

Appreciating a helpful act can support its return. The emphasis is on continued conduct, not collecting a score.
