Wise effort · Dhp 122

Drop by drop

A water pot fills through individual drops. The verse compares this with a wise person gathering goodness through small actions.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What does the filling pot show?

A closer look

  1. Small good actions accumulate

Small does not mean meaningless. Repeated helpful choices can shape a direction over time without making a spiritual score.
