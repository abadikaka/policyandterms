Wise effort · Dhp 112

Energy with purpose

The verse values a day of firm, purposeful effort over many years of drifting without effort. The invitation is to begin practicing.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is valued?

A closer look

  1. Purposeful effort

Purposeful effort can be small and sustainable. The verse is not a measure of worth based on health, ability, or productivity.
