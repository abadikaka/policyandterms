Speech & listening · Dhp 259

Carry it in your life

Knowing many words does not alone make someone a bearer of the teaching. Even limited learning matters when a person sees and practices it carefully.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

How is the teaching truly carried?

A closer look

Dhammapada • Dhp 259
