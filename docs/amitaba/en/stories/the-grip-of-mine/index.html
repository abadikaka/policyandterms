Change & letting go · Dhp 62

The grip of mine

The verse pictures distress built around thoughts of my children and my wealth. It questions how completely anything can be possessed, even oneself.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is being questioned?

A closer look

  1. The grip of possessiveness

The target is ownership-like clinging, not love or responsibility. We can care deeply without treating people as possessions.
