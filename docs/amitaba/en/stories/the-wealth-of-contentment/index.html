Change & letting go · Dhp 204

The wealth of contentment

The verse calls contentment a great wealth and trust a valuable relationship, while naming Nibbāna the highest happiness.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is pictured as a kind of wealth?

A closer look

  1. Contentment

Contentment can mean recognizing enough while still meeting genuine needs. The verse is not a medical guarantee or a demand to accept injustice.
