Change & letting go · Dhp 152

Growing in understanding

The verse distinguishes growing older from growing in understanding. Time passing does not by itself provide the learning of a thoughtful life.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

A closer look

  1. Growth in understanding

The invitation is to keep learning. It does not rank people by education or dismiss those with different abilities.
