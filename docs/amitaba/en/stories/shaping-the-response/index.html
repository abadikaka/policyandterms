Careful attention · Dhp 80

A skill that can grow

Water workers guide water, arrow makers shape arrows, and carpenters shape wood. The verse compares these crafts with a wise person's self-training.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What do the three crafts illustrate?

A closer look

  1. Training one's own responses

Craft develops through patient work with real conditions. So can the way we respond to a difficult moment.
