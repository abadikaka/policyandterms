Generosity & conduct · Dhp 231

Let the body help

This verse asks us to guard against harmful bodily action and cultivate good conduct through the body. Restraint becomes something visible.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is paired with avoiding harmful action?

A closer look

  1. Practicing good bodily conduct

Care can become practical: stepping back, handling something gently, or offering useful help. The verse gives restraint an active side.
