Friendship · Dhp 61

Choose company carefully

The verse advises a traveler to go alone if no suitable companion is found. Mere company is not worth adopting an unwise direction.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What matters more than simply having company?

A closer look

  1. The quality of companionship

This is discernment about influence, not a command to isolate. Helpful friends, teachers, and support remain valuable.
