Kindness · Dhp 201

Beyond winning and losing

Victory can create resentment and defeat can bring distress. The verse points to peace beyond being caught in winning and losing.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is being loosened?

A closer look

  1. Dependence on victory and defeat

Not every conversation needs a winner. We can still seek a fair outcome without treating another's humiliation as success.
