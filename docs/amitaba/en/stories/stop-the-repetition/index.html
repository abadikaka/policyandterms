Wise effort · Dhp 117

One mistake need not become a pattern

After doing harm, a person is urged not to repeat or delight in it. The verse points toward interrupting the pattern.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is the next useful direction after harm?

A closer look

  1. Stop repeating it

Acknowledging a mistake can support repair and change. The verse is about abandoning harmful conduct, not endless self-condemnation.
