Friendship · Dhp 328–329

A wise traveling companion

The verses offer two possibilities: travel gladly and mindfully with a wise companion, or go alone if such company is not available.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What determines the choice?

A closer look

  1. Whether the companion is wise and good

The passage values friendship without making company an absolute requirement. Discernment matters more than pressure to belong.

Dhammapada • Dhp 328–329

Sace labhetha nipakaṁ sahāyaṁ, Saddhiṁ caraṁ sādhuvihāridhīraṁ; Abhibhuyya sabbāni parissayāni, Careyya tenattamano satīmā. No ce labhetha nipakaṁ sahāyaṁ, Saddhiṁ caraṁ sādhuvihāridhīraṁ; Rājāva raṭṭhaṁ vijitaṁ pahāya, Eko care mātaṅgaraññeva nāgo.
