Careful attention · Dhp 27

A different kind of joy

This verse contrasts careless pursuit of sensual enjoyment with the happiness of heedful meditation. It points beyond chasing the next pleasant sensation.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which source of happiness is emphasized?

A closer look

  1. Heedful meditation

The contrast concerns what we rely on for happiness. It need not become hostility toward the body or ordinary care.
