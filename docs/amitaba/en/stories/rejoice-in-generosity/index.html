Everyday practice · Dhp 177

Rejoice in giving

The verse contrasts withholding and dismissing generosity with the wise person's appreciation of giving. Joy can be directed toward another's good act.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What response to generosity is encouraged?

A closer look

  1. Appreciating and rejoicing in it

Generosity can be supported without competing. Appreciating someone else's help can loosen comparison and possessiveness.
