Everyday practice · Dhp 160

Become a reliable support

The verse asks who else can be one's protector if one does not train oneself. A well-trained person becomes a valuable support to themselves.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What creates a reliable inner support?

A closer look

  1. Careful self-training

Personal responsibility does not cancel interdependence. The emphasis is on developing the part of care that only you can practice.
