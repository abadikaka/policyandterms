Change & letting go · Dhp 186–187

When more is not enough

The verses say even a shower of coins cannot fully satisfy craving for sensual pleasures. They invite examining the hunger for more itself.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What remains unsatisfied by more wealth?

A closer look

  1. Craving for sensual pleasure

The distinction is between meeting needs and expecting acquisition to end craving. These are not the same project.
