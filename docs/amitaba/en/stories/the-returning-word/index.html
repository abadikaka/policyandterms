Speech & listening · Dhp 133

A harsh word returns

Harsh speech can invite a harsh reply. This verse asks the speaker to notice that words can hurt and feed an escalating exchange.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What risk does the verse identify?

A closer look

  1. Harshness feeding further harshness

A firm boundary can be expressed without a personal attack. The teaching concerns harmful speech, not forced agreement.
