Wise effort · Dhp 110–111

The quality of a day

These verses compare a long life without ethical care or wisdom with a single day lived with virtue, reflection, and understanding.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What matters in this comparison?

A closer look

  1. The quality of how life is lived

The point is to value meaningful practice now. The comparison does not make anyone's life less worthy of care.

Dhammapada • Dhp 110–111

Yo ca vassasataṁ jīve, dussīlo asamāhito; Ekāhaṁ jīvitaṁ seyyo, sīlavantassa jhāyino. Yo ca vassasataṁ jīve, duppañño asamāhito; Ekāhaṁ jīvitaṁ seyyo, paññavantassa jhāyino.
