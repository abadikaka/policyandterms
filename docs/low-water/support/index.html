Salt House · Low Water

Game support

Low Water is a single-player survival-horror adventure set at Saint Osyth pumping station.

Movement and machinery

Play in landscape. Drag on the left to walk and on the right to look. Tap the action beside a wheel or door, then lift your movement thumb so Mara can take hold. Move again to break away.

Getting your bearings

Open Pause, then Notes & map. The station plan shows your position and next objective. The flood register preserves information from your investigation. Settings includes captions, volume and look sensitivity.

Checkpoints

The game saves at major discoveries and machinery changes. After a fall, choose Retry from checkpoint. To begin a fresh story, choose Start over in the main menu. Confirming replaces your story progress.
