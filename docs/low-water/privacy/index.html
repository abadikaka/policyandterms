Salt House · Low Water

Privacy

Effective November 15, 2026

Low Water is an offline survival-horror game for iPhone and iPad. You can play the whole story without an account or an internet connection.

On your device

The game saves your checkpoint, discovered evidence, completion state, volume, captions, and look sensitivity on your device. Start over from the main menu to replace your current story progress. Deleting the app removes its local save and settings. Any device backup you have enabled is managed by Apple.

Data and tracking

Low Water does not send gameplay, identifying information, or analytics to the developer. It contains no ads or tracking software and does not request access to your microphone, contacts, camera or location.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, uses them to answer your request, and does not sell them. You may request deletion of a support message by contacting the same address. Links you open in a browser are subject to that site’s privacy practices.
