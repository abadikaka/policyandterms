LOW WATER
Voice and sound credits

Mara, Elsie, Abel Bell and the coast operator are fictional characters. All dialogue was written for Low Water. Their voices are synthetic. The named corpus contributors did not perform these roles.

Mara
Rendered with the Alba medium voice. Cassia Valentini-Botinhao and Junichi Yamagishi, Alba speech corpus, University of Edinburgh, 2019. The model was fine-tuned from hifi_tts_92, trained on reader 92 in NVIDIA’s Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English TTS Dataset (E. Bakhturina, V. Lavrukhin, B. Ginsburg and Y. Zhang, 2021). Both corpora are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The game uses the generated phrases with equalisation and a short room reflection.
https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2506
https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/bitstreams/ca0b398a-635c-48cd-b85a-beb6f8401e0d/download
https://www.openslr.org/109/

Elsie
Rendered with the LJ Speech high voice, trained from scratch on the public-domain LJ Speech Dataset by Keith Ito and Linda Johnson. Her phrases were edited with original tape hiss, tape-speed variation and station reflections.
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/

Abel Bell and the coast operator
Rendered with two individual voices from LibriTTS high (speaker indices 3 and 5, corpus IDs 6701 and 922). The model was trained from scratch on LibriTTS train-clean-360. Heiga Zen, Viet Dang, Rob Clark, Yu Zhang, Ron J. Weiss, Ye Jia, Zhifeng Chen and Yonghui Wu, “LibriTTS: A Corpus Derived from LibriSpeech for Text-to-Speech,” 2019. Corpus supplied under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Original phrases were edited, gain-matched and filtered separately for archival tape and marine radio.
https://www.openslr.org/60/
https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2019-2441

The Occupied Line
The cast and dialogue in Chapters 2–20 were created for Low Water. Mara uses the Alba voice above. The Caller's borrowed voice uses LJ Speech. June (8699), Nell (3638), Toby (3922), Ruth (4535), Pip (8848), Tom (6701), the clerk (3615), Coast (922), pilot Len (2531), and Mrs Dean (1638) use the LibriTTS high voice and its corpus cited above. These synthetic roles were rendered offline with Piper, edited for level and room tone, and compressed for mobile playback.

Voice production

Sound design
All seven room tones, memorial bell strikes, tape transport, radio squelch, mooring sounds, launch motor and distant shore-bird calls were synthesised specifically for this production. The original game dialogue, sound design and edits use no third-party music or location recordings.
