Controls

Move with the thumbstick. Basic attacks automatically target enemies in range when ready. Tap Dodge and either of your two weapon skills to avoid attacks and protect the crystal. Left-handed controls and audio, haptic, text, and effects options are available in settings.

Free chapter and purchases

The four Verdant Gate missions are free. Full Adventure is a one-time purchase for Crystal Depths, Ashen Keep, the campaign ending, and Challenge mode after completing the campaign. Guardian Supporter Pack is an optional cosmetic purchase for Full Adventure owners. Neither purchase is a subscription.

Restore a purchase

Connect to the internet, use the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Adventure Store from the hub, and choose Restore Purchases. A pending purchase may require approval before content unlocks. If ownership is missing, contact us with your app version and what happened. Do not send payment-card information or your Apple Account password.

Progress and interruptions

Completed waves bank rewards and a checkpoint. An interrupted expedition resumes from its next unbanked wave. Gameplay progress stays on the current device and does not synchronize across devices. Deleting the app may remove progress. Restoring purchases does not restore a campaign save.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, mission, weapon, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots or a voluntary diagnostic export can help. Avoid attaching unrelated personal information.
