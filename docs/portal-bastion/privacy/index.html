Portal Bastion Privacy Policy

Michael Abadi Santoso · Effective September 21, 2026

Portal Bastion is a solo action-defense game. The developer does not collect gameplay data through the app. No account is required.

Data on your device

Campaign progress, equipment, companions, currencies, checkpoints, and preferences are stored locally. Gameplay progress does not synchronize between devices. Removing the app can remove its local saves; device backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchases, unlock content, and restore ownership. Verified ownership is stored separately from gameplay saves for offline access. We do not receive payment-card details or operate a purchase-processing server. Apple’s privacy policy and purchase terms apply to its services.

No tracking or advertising

The app contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics service, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated gameplay analytics backend. Apple may provide system diagnostics according to your device and sharing settings.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and information you choose to send, including voluntarily attached diagnostic exports. We use it to respond and investigate your request and retain it only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated personal information. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, or cookies. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices

You can adjust game settings, remove local app data by deleting the app, and restore eligible purchases through Adventure Store using the Apple Account that purchased them. Restoration restores ownership, not gameplay progress.

Changes and contact

Updates will appear here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, contact abadi0067@gmail.com.
