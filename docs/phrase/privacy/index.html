Phrase Me Better privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Phrase Me Better, called Phrase inside the app, helps you practice English. This policy explains the information used by the app and by the services that support purchases.

Learning information

Your practice answers, attempts, saved material, preferences, and unfinished sessions are stored on your device. Phrase does not require an app account. This release does not provide an app-operated cloud service for learning data, and learning data is not sent to RevenueCat.

You can export your learning data in Settings. Local data remains on the device until it is removed with the app or device data. Apple device backups may retain copies according to your device and iCloud settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Optional microphone and speech access

When you choose speaking practice, Phrase requests permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Recognition uses supported on-device Apple capabilities. Apple may need to download an English speech model. If recognition is unavailable or permission is denied, you can use self-review or skip speaking.

Phrase does not upload your recordings to a cloud speech-recognition service or to RevenueCat. Temporary recordings are deleted after feedback or when practice is canceled. Speech feedback compares recognized words; it does not assess your accent or provide a pronunciation grade. You can change microphone and speech permissions in device Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. Phrase does not receive your payment-card details.

RevenueCat helps verify purchases, restore access, and understand subscription activity. It receives purchase history and a randomly generated anonymous app identifier. Phrase does not provide RevenueCat with your learning answers, recordings, name, or email address. We do not use purchase information for advertising tracking or connect the anonymous identifier to your contact details.

Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide subscription access, restoration, and their related services. Deleting the app does not erase their purchase records. Their own policies explain their handling of this information:

  • Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Reminders and links

Optional reminders use the notification permissions you choose on your device. You can change notification permissions in device Settings. Opening an external link, such as the teacher's professional profile or Apple's subscription management, takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer your request and resolve issues. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or audio recordings containing information you do not want to share. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and any related obligations.

Your choices and contact

You can use free lessons without an app account, skip speaking, export local learning data, adjust device permissions, and manage subscriptions through Apple. For questions about this policy or information you have sent to support, contact abadi0067@gmail.com.

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The policy's effective date will identify the latest version.
