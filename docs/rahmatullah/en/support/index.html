A little help along the way

For technical help or content corrections, email us.

abadi0067@gmail.com

Include your app version, iPhone model, the lesson or screen, and what happened. For a content concern, include the reference and exact wording. Please do not send personal reflections or sensitive religious information.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions and Rahmatullah. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore access?

Use Restore purchases on the opening screen with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Progress is local and does not sync to another device.

Does it work offline?

Bundled lessons and saved progress work offline while a verified subscription remains active. Purchasing, restoring, source links, and downloading updates require a connection.

How do monthly lessons arrive?

Install the latest Rahmatullah update from the App Store. New lessons receive the same source, language, and editorial review process before inclusion.

Can I request a refund?

Apple handles billing and refund eligibility. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com for a refund request.

What if my progress cannot be opened?

The app preserves the original file. Its recovery option starts a new journey while retaining a local backup. Device backups may also retain app data.
