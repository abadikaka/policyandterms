Rahmatullah membership terms · 11 October 2026

Rahmatullah offers 100 Quran-based lessons in English, Bahasa Indonesia, and Arabic, with short challenges, explanations, optional reflections, and symbolic illustrations. An active annual subscription is required to access lessons. New reviewed lessons are added monthly through app updates; keep the app updated to receive them. Lessons are educational notes, not personalized religious rulings or a complete Quran translation.

The price shown by the App Store for your region applies. The full annual amount is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. There is no free trial. The subscription renews automatically each year unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues for the paid period unless Apple refunds or revokes it. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account to recover access on another supported device. Learning progress is local and does not sync between devices. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its policies and applicable law.

You can use bundled lessons offline while a verified subscription remains active. Purchasing, restoring, and downloading updates need an internet connection. If access expires, your local progress is kept. We may correct or update lessons and artwork to improve accuracy. No spiritual outcome is guaranteed.

Apple’s standard license agreement applies. Your mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected. Contact abadi0067@gmail.com for app support or content corrections.
