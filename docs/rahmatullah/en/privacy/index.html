Rahmatullah privacy policy · 11 October 2026

Rahmatullah is provided by Michael Abadi Santoso. Learning progress, the current lesson checkpoint, and your chosen language stay on your device. The checkpoint may include selected challenge choices so an interrupted lesson can resume. We do not send these choices or learning progress to our servers. There are no accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or personal journals. Reflections are optional prompts; there is no field for recording them.

Apple processes purchases and provides the subscription status that the app verifies through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

Device backups may include local app data. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading it may retain data. Existing backups must be managed separately in your Apple settings. If a saved progress file cannot be read, it is preserved; choosing to start again keeps a backup on the device.

External links open services with their own privacy practices. Our public website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process connection information such as IP addresses. We place no tracking cookies or analytics scripts on our app pages.

If you email support, we receive your address and the details you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request, retaining correspondence only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send private reflections or sensitive religious information. Contact abadi0067@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Updates to this policy will appear on our website.
