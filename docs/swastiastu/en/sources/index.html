Sources & editorial notes

Swastiastu’s initial library is a focused journey through 100 distinct passages of the Bhagavad Gita. It is not a survey of all Hindu traditions. Hindu schools interpret the Gita in different ways; these concise notes introduce selected teachings without claiming to settle those differences.

The original Sanskrit passages are reproduced unchanged from the pinned gita/gita source archive, released under the Unlicense. The exact source file and license notice are bundled. References follow that source edition, including its chapter 13 numbering. No modern published translation, transliteration, or word-by-word commentary is bundled. English, Bahasa Indonesia, and Hindi summaries and explanations are original learning notes, not verbatim translations. The source panel provides the full included Sanskrit passage, reference, and context. Consult a reliable edition and a qualified teacher for deeper interpretation.

The Gita’s dialogue is set on a battlefield. Its historical and literary context is explained; lessons do not instruct modern violence. Illustrations are symbolic, not historical reconstructions. Everyday dilemmas are fictional applications, with optional, ungraded perspectives; personal choices are not spiritual scores. The publisher reviews release content; we do not claim independent scholarly endorsement. Send corrections with the lesson title and exact wording to abadi0067@gmail.com.
