Seeing with Evenness

The yogin sees the self in all beings and all beings in the self. The verse describes an evenness of vision.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

How broad is this vision?

A closer look

  1. It includes all beings

The metaphysics of this vision are interpreted differently across Hindu schools. Its breadth can prompt reflection on dignity beyond one’s familiar circle.
