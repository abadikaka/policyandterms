Life Is Active

Krishna observes that nobody remains entirely without action even for a moment. The qualities of nature keep embodied life moving.

What does Krishna say about complete inactivity?

A closer look

  1. No one sustains it even momentarily

The point challenges a simple equation of spirituality with inactivity. It prepares the teaching about how to act rather than whether life involves action.

Bhagavad Gita 3:5

न हि कश्िचत्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत्। कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः।।3.5।।

Passage context

Chapter three explores karma yoga after Arjuna asks about knowledge and action. Krishna discusses necessary action, sacrifice, example and lokasangraha. Modern applications here are suggestions for reflection; they do not erase the dialogue’s battlefield setting or settle every question of duty.
