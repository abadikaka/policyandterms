Three Destructive Pulls

Bhagavad Gita 16:21

त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः।कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत्।।16.21।।

Passage context

Chapters fourteen, sixteen and seventeen describe the gunas, ethical qualities and forms of discipline. These are philosophical and religious categories, not diagnoses or fixed labels for people. Observe a pattern’s effects while avoiding claims of superiority over another person.
