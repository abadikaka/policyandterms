Passage context

Chapter ten presents vibhutis, examples of divine manifestation, followed by Arjuna’s request for the universal form in chapter eleven. These theological images belong to the Gita’s dialogue. Symbolic artwork in the app is not a canonical depiction of a deity or a reconstruction of the vision.
