Compassionate Character · 12:14

Resolve with Contentment

Contentment, self-restraint and firm resolve accompany a mind and understanding offered to Krishna.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which quality accompanies contentment?

A closer look

  1. Firm resolve

Contentment here coexists with dedication. It need not mean losing interest in growth or neglecting a responsibility.
