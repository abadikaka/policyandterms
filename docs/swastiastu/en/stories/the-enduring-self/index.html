Bhagavad Gita 2:20

न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचि न्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः। अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे।।2.20।।

Passage context

Chapter two moves from Arjuna’s grief to teachings on the self, action and steady wisdom. These notes offer an introductory reading. Hindu schools differ in their accounts of the self, liberation and the relation to the divine; this journey does not speak for every tradition.

Summaries and explanations are original learning notes, not scripture translations. Interpretations vary between traditions.
