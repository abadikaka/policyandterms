Respect in Giving

A gift offered at an unsuitable time or place, without respect or with contempt, is described as tamasic.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What attitude makes the gift problematic?

A closer look

  1. Contempt

Giving can harm dignity when it humiliates the recipient. This verse invites attention to manner and context, not only the thing transferred.
