Reflect and Act · 17:20

Giving Without a Return

A sattvic gift is given because giving is fitting, without expecting repayment, with attention to recipient, place and time.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What expectation is set aside?

A closer look

  1. Repayment

Generosity includes discernment and respect. The teaching concerns how to give well, not permission to rank people’s human worth.
