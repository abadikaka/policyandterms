Devotion in Daily Life · 8:7

Remembering While Acting

Krishna asks Arjuna to remember him while fulfilling his battlefield responsibility. Attention and action are brought together.

A closer look

  1. Remembrance of Krishna

The war setting must remain visible. For daily reflection, consider remembrance within responsible work; do not generalize the battlefield command into permission to harm.

Passage context

These passages from chapters seven to twelve explore Krishna’s devotional teaching. Bhakti has many expressions across Hindu traditions. Examples of remembrance and offering are invitations for reflection, not instructions to replace a reader’s community, tradition or established practice.
