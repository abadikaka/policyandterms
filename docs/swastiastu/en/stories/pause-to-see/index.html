Bhagavad Gita 1:21

अर्जुन उवाच हृषीकेशं तदा वाक्यमिदमाह महीपते। सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये रथं स्थापय मेऽच्युत।।1.21।।

Passage context

The Bhagavad Gita is a dialogue within the Mahabharata, set before the battle of Kurukshetra. Arjuna faces relatives and teachers in the opposing armies. These early verses present his crisis; they are not the complete teaching or a general instruction to wage war.
