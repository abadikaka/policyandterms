Attention and Action

Which combination is commended?

A closer look

  1. Self-restraint and unattached action

The surrounding contrast is with outward restraint while the mind dwells on objects. Practice involves attention as well as visible behavior.

यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन। कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते।।3.7।।

Passage context

Chapter three explores karma yoga after Arjuna asks about knowledge and action. Krishna discusses necessary action, sacrifice, example and lokasangraha. Modern applications here are suggestions for reflection; they do not erase the dialogue’s battlefield setting or settle every question of duty.
