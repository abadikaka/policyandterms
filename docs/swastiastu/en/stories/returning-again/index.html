Returning Again

When the restless mind wanders, Krishna instructs the practitioner to bring it back. Returning is part of the practice.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What follows noticing that the mind has wandered?

A closer look

  1. Bringing it back

The instruction anticipates wandering. A repeated return can be patient and deliberate rather than a verdict of failure.
