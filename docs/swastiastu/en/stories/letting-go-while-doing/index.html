Reflect and Act · 18:9

Letting Go While Doing

Sattvic renunciation is described as doing necessary work because it should be done, while giving up attachment and its fruit.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What is relinquished here?

A closer look

  1. Attachment and the fruit of action

Renunciation is presented through the way of acting. It does not simply mean abandoning tasks that other people depend on.
