Passage context

These passages from chapters seven to twelve explore Krishna’s devotional teaching. Bhakti has many expressions across Hindu traditions. Examples of remembrance and offering are invitations for reflection, not instructions to replace a reader’s community, tradition or established practice.

Summaries and explanations are original learning notes, not scripture translations. Interpretations vary between traditions.
