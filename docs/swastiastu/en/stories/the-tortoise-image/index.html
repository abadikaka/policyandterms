Steady Wisdom · 2:58

The Tortoise Image

A tortoise draws its limbs inward. Krishna compares this with gathering the senses away from their objects.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

Which creature illustrates sensory restraint?

A closer look

  1. A tortoise

The image concerns the capacity to withdraw attention. A small pause from stimulation can support choice rather than automatic reaction.

Passage context

Chapter two moves from Arjuna’s grief to teachings on the self, action and steady wisdom. These notes offer an introductory reading. Hindu schools differ in their accounts of the self, liberation and the relation to the divine; this journey does not speak for every tradition.
