Reflect and Act · 18:11

Not Every Action Can Stop

Krishna says an embodied person cannot abandon all action completely. The relinquishing of action’s fruits is central to renunciation here.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What cannot be abandoned completely while embodied?

A closer look

  1. All action

The verse returns to the distinction between action and attachment. Choosing well still matters even when total inactivity is impossible.
