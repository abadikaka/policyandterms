Same Work, New Purpose

Krishna contrasts action driven by attachment with wise action for the world’s welfare. The outward activity may look similar.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What distinguishes the wise person’s purpose?

A closer look

  1. Supporting the world without attachment

The difference lies in motivation and attachment, not simply in being busy. Wisdom is meant to shape participation in common life.

सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत। कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्िचकीर्षुर्लोकसंग्रहम्।।3.25।।

Passage context

Chapter three explores karma yoga after Arjuna asks about knowledge and action. Krishna discusses necessary action, sacrifice, example and lokasangraha. Modern applications here are suggestions for reflection; they do not erase the dialogue’s battlefield setting or settle every question of duty.
