Seeing the Divine · 10:20

Within All Beings

Krishna identifies himself as the self dwelling in the hearts of all beings, and as their beginning, middle and end.

A closer look

  1. In the hearts of all beings

This verse frames the examples of divine manifestation that follow. Its scope includes ordinary living beings, not only impressive sights.

Passage context

Chapter ten presents vibhutis, examples of divine manifestation, followed by Arjuna’s request for the universal form in chapter eleven. These theological images belong to the Gita’s dialogue. Symbolic artwork in the app is not a canonical depiction of a deity or a reconstruction of the vision.
