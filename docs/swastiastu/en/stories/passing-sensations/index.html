Steady Wisdom · 2:14

What Comes and Goes

Heat and cold, pleasure and pain arise through contact with the senses. Krishna emphasizes that these experiences come and go.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What quality do these sensations share?

A closer look

  1. They are temporary

Recognizing change supports endurance. It does not require ignoring injury or refusing practical help.

Passage context

Chapter two moves from Arjuna’s grief to teachings on the self, action and steady wisdom. These notes offer an introductory reading. Hindu schools differ in their accounts of the self, liberation and the relation to the divine; this journey does not speak for every tradition.
