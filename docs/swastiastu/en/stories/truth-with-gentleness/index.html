Truth with Gentleness

Passage context

The qualities of a devotee in chapter twelve are read beside the ethical lists in chapters thirteen and sixteen. Chapter thirteen’s verse numbers differ across editions; this app consistently follows the bundled Sanskrit edition. Character descriptions are ideals for reflection, not scores for judging people.
