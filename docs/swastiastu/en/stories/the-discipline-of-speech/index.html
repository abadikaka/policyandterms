Clear Choices · 17:15

The Discipline of Speech

Speech that does not needlessly distress, that is truthful, kind and beneficial, is joined with study in the discipline of speech.

Editorial summary · Not a scripture translation

What accompanies truthfulness?

A closer look

  1. Kindness and benefit

The qualities are held together. Pleasant words alone are not enough if misleading; truth alone does not justify needless injury.

Bhagavad Gita 17:15

अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत्।स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते।।17.15।।

Passage context

Chapters fourteen, sixteen and seventeen describe the gunas, ethical qualities and forms of discipline. These are philosophical and religious categories, not diagnoses or fixed labels for people. Observe a pattern’s effects while avoiding claims of superiority over another person.

Summaries and explanations are original learning notes, not scripture translations. Interpretations vary between traditions.
