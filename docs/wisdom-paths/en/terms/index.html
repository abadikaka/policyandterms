Wisdom Paths terms of use

Wisdom Paths offers a yearly auto-renewing membership for the full 400-lesson library and its interactive activities. New reviewed lessons are planned monthly through app updates. The membership price and local currency are shown by Apple before purchase; the US annual price is US$29.

Your Apple Account is charged when you confirm a purchase and before each yearly renewal. The subscription renews until cancelled. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in Apple subscription settings. Access continues through the paid period. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

Lessons are for personal learning and reflection, not a substitute for a religious teacher or community. The four traditions are presented in separate journeys; Question Compass offers curated entry points without claiming that their teachings are equivalent.

Scripture and source-text attributions and license notices appear with each lesson. Learning notes, challenges, and artwork are separate from the quoted source text. Please do not republish the source text or artwork from the app without checking the applicable rights.

Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies. Contact support for help with app access or content corrections.
