Wisdom Paths privacy policy

Wisdom Paths is provided by Michael Abadi Santoso. The app stores your chosen journey and language, completed and saved lessons, Gospel recall dates, saved Gita comparison pairs, and Buddhist practice visit dates on your device. It does not store your challenge answers or personal reflections.

We do not operate app accounts, advertising, or third-party analytics. The app does not send your learning progress to our servers. Local app data may be included in device backups. Deleting the app removes its local data; existing backups must be managed separately in your Apple settings.

Apple processes membership purchases and provides subscription status through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies and device settings.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process connection information such as IP addresses. We use no analytics scripts or tracking cookies on these pages. External source links have their own privacy practices.

If you email abadi0067@gmail.com, we receive your address and the details you send. We use them to answer your request and retain correspondence only as needed for support or legal obligations. Please do not send private reflections or sensitive religious information.
