Wisdom Paths support

For purchase, content, accessibility, or privacy questions, email abadi0067@gmail.com. Include the app version, your iPhone model, and a short description of the problem. Please do not send personal reflections or payment-card details.

If a membership does not unlock the library, use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account that made the purchase. For billing or cancellation, open your Apple subscription settings.

A saved progress file that cannot be read is kept on-device until you explicitly archive and reset it. You may switch among the four journeys without deleting progress.
