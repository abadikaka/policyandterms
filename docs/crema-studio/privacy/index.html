Crema 1:2 Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Crema 1:2 is operated by Michael Abadi Santoso. This policy describes version 1.0, including its TestFlight beta.

Your coffee practice stays on your device

Crema 1:2 stores simulated brews, saved recipes, lesson and challenge progress, preferences, and recovery checkpoints on your device. The latest latte cup includes its simulated milk marks and input settings. This version does not sync these records to iCloud or a developer server. You do not need a Crema 1:2 account.

Tilt and motion

Device gravity is processed on your device to control virtual milk flow. Raw motion readings are transient; they are not saved or sent to the developer. Turn off Tilt to pour to use touch controls. The app does not request microphone, GPS location, or contacts access.

Photographing and collecting real coffees

In versions that include My coffee finds, you may grant camera permission to photograph a coffee package or capture multiple views for a 3D scan on a supported device. You may also choose an individual image through Apple’s photo picker; the app does not request access to your entire photo library. A single photograph creates a simple 3D display, not a reconstruction of unseen surfaces. Multi-angle reconstruction runs on your device through Apple’s Object Capture technology.

Your chosen images, capture photographs, 3D models, unfinished capture draft, and details you enter—such as coffee name, country, roaster, tasting notes, price, shop address and shop link—are stored locally. These are not uploaded to the developer, Meshy, or another generation service. The app does not use GPS to fill buying locations. Your personal collection does not sync between devices in this version. Device backups may include local files, depending on your Apple settings.

You can edit or remove individual saved coffees. Removing a coffee removes its local model and photo. Discarding a capture draft removes its local working photographs. Exporting a model opens Apple’s share sheet and sends the file only to the destination you choose; the destination’s policies apply. Exports and backups can remain after local deletion. Opening a saved shop link contacts that website.

Sharing a cup

Only when you choose Share your cup does Crema 1:2 open the system share sheet with an image of your completed cup. You choose the recipient or destination. That destination's privacy practices apply. Exported images may remain in the destination after you delete the app.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Studio Unlock and Collector Plus Lifetime one-time purchases through the App Store. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions to provide and restore purchased features. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Purchase ownership is separate from locally saved progress. Three saved personal coffees are free; Collector Plus Lifetime permanently removes the personal collection limit without recurring payments. Following a refund or revoked entitlement, existing coffees remain viewable, editable, exportable and removable. Restoring ownership does not transfer locally stored coffee photos or models. Collector Plus is separate from Studio Unlock. Gems are earned in this version and used for Passport beans and cosmetics, not personal collection slots. TestFlight purchases use Apple's testing environment.

No advertising or tracking

Crema 1:2 contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or developer telemetry endpoint. We do not sell your practice data. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, device backup, and diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. The Meshy service was used during asset production; the installed app does not send player content to Meshy or another AI service.

Support and retention

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending passwords or payment details. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any records we must retain by law.

Local practice records remain in the app's storage until the app is deleted through system settings. Offloading the app can retain data. Device backups and App Store purchase history are controlled by Apple and are not necessarily deleted when you remove the app.

This policy website

These static policy and support pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. The host may process ordinary web request information to deliver the pages. No analytics or advertising scripts are embedded in these pages.
