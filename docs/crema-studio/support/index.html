Your first espresso

Open Studio and choose Guide my first shot. Grind, distribute, tamp, and lock in, then start brewing. Watch the scale and stop near 36 grams. The feedback explains your simulated result. Equipment and coffee profiles are fictional; the app does not control a real machine.

Tilt to pour latte art

Open Latte Art. Hold the device comfortably while it calibrates, then choose Start pouring. Tip the top edge away from your resting position to increase milk flow, and bring it back to stop. Drag on the cup to move the spout. High mixes the milk into the coffee; Low leaves a white pattern on the surface.

Use Calibrate after changing your grip. If tilt is unavailable or uncomfortable, turn off Tilt to pour and use Milk flow. Fine position controls offer an alternative to dragging. Pause, Finish, and New cup are always available as appropriate. Guides are outlines, not automatic completed designs.

Interruptions and device layouts

Practice adapts to iPhone, iPhone Duo, and iPad windows. Leaving the app pauses the pour; resume when ready. The latest cup and active espresso checkpoint are local. Version 1.0 saves progress on the device and does not provide iCloud synchronization.

Studio Unlock and restore

The free starter includes latte art and unlimited starter espresso practice. Studio Unlock is a permanent purchase for all launch espresso equipment, beans, lessons, challenges, comparisons, and customization. It is not a subscription. Future expansions may be sold separately.

To restore, connect to the internet, use the Apple Account that made the purchase, and open Studio settings → Restore Purchases. Pending purchases do not unlock content until approved. Restoring a purchase does not restore deleted local progress.

Collector Plus Lifetime

Collection → My coffee finds holds your personal coffee packages and buying notes. Three saved coffees are free. Collector Plus Lifetime permanently removes that limit with a one-time purchase. Restore Purchases restores ownership with the same Apple Account; it does not transfer photos or models between devices. Gems unlock Passport beans and cosmetics, not personal collection slots. Camera scanning depends on hardware support; a single photograph creates a simple 3D display.
